User Account & Data Deletion Request

Overview of Account Deletion & Privacy

Gate Track is designed as a privacy-first, community-driven railway gate status tracking application. The application does not require users to create an account using passwords or email addresses. Instead, client installations interact with the system using pseudonymous, cryptographically hashed client tokens.

Users have the right at any time to permanently purge their client identifiers, revoke active sessions, delete user-submitted reports, and erase all locally cached railway information.

Option 1: In-App Instant Deletion (Recommended)

You can permanently delete your client account and all device-associated data instantly within the mobile app:

  1. Open the Gate Track mobile application on your device.
  2. Navigate to the Settings screen (gear icon in the top/bottom bar).
  3. Scroll down to the Community & Legal section.
  4. Tap on Delete Account & Stored Data.
  5. Review the confirmation dialog detailing the data removal.
  6. Tap Delete Everything to execute the permanent purge.

Once initiated, the app immediately revokes all authentication tokens on the server, deletes active transit tracking sessions, clears push notification tokens, wipes the local encrypted database (SQLCipher), and resets the app to a clean state.

Option 2: External Deletion Request (Web / Email)

If you have already uninstalled the application or cannot access your mobile device, you can submit a deletion request directly to our developer team:

  • Email: railgatecheck@gmail.com
  • Subject: Gate Track Account and Data Deletion Request
  • Required Details: If known, provide your anonymous Client Public ID (visible in Settings -> Account or prior crash logs) or your Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration token.

External deletion requests are verified and processed within 7 business days.

Data Retention & Exceptions

Public Safety Notice: When an account is deleted, personal linkages and user identifiers are permanently scrubbed from historical crossing status logs. Anonymized aggregate status indicators (e.g., historical record that Gate #42 was observed closed at 14:00) are retained strictly to preserve railway safety consensus models and train arrival prediction algorithms.

Summary of Deletion Actions

Data Category Action Taken Upon Deletion
Client Tokens & HMAC Keys Permanently revoked and cleared from device storage and server state.
Active Transit Sessions Immediately terminated and associated GPS observations deleted.
Gate Issue Reports & Feedback Permanently purged from administrative records.
Report Photos & Support Chat Images Permanently deleted, including the stored copies in the private Discord channels.
Push Notification Subscriptions Unsubscribed and device token associations deleted.
Local Offline Cache (SQLCipher) All cached gates, tracks, and pending reports erased immediately on device.
